About

We study the future
before it arrives.

FutureLens is a student-led science collaboration group committed to exploring emerging fields — from biotechnology and AI to environmental science and beyond.

We believe science is best done together. Our members come from different disciplines, bringing unique perspectives to every question we investigate.

Through research, experiments, and open discussion, we work to understand the forces shaping our world — and imagine better ones.
